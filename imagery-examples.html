2

Human photography with a brand duotone

You sell to NPOs — show the people they serve. A green duotone overlay keeps every photo on-brand and consistent, so stock photography never looks like clip-art. Compare a raw photo (left) vs duotone-treated (middle/right).

Raw stock — off-brand
Duotone green — on-brand
Works on any source photo

CSS recipe for a real photo: filter:grayscale(1) contrast(1.1); on the <img> + an absolutely-positioned overlay background:linear-gradient(135deg,#007860,#0EB689); mix-blend-mode:multiply; opacity:.7;. Use these in the Problem, Audience and Impact sections.

3

Custom line icons, not emoji

Emoji render differently on every device and instantly signal "template." One consistent SVG icon set in your green is the highest design-credibility-per-effort change you can make.

🗂️

Before — emoji

Dự án / Projects

Single stroke weight, brand green, pixel-perfect at any size.

Báo cáo / Reports

Matches the chart in your stats — visual language stays unified.

Free, consistent sets that match this style: Lucide, Phosphor, or Heroicons (outline). Set stroke:var(--primary) and one stroke-width globally so all six modules feel like a family.

4

Turn "127+ organizations" into a logo strip

A number tells; logos prove. A greyscale partner/NPO strip below the hero or stats bar gives instant credibility. Greyscale keeps it calm and on-brand.

Use real partner logos at ~28px height, filter:grayscale(1);opacity:.55; and fade to full color on hover. If logos aren't cleared for use, keep it as anonymized "sector" badges instead.

5

Give flat sections depth with texture

Your white/mint sections are flat. Soft brand-color gradient "blobs" and a faint grid add depth without competing with content. Subtle is the whole point.

6 phân hệ, 1 hệ sinh thái

The same section copy — now sitting on a surface with subtle depth instead of flat white.

Two absolutely-positioned blurred divs + a faint CSS background-grid. Costs nothing in performance and works behind any section. Keep opacity low (<0.5) so text contrast stays AA.
